## Positioning Statement

Fill in the template:

> For **[ICP description]** who **[primary pain/need]**, **[Product Name]** is the **[category]** that **[primary differentiator]**. Unlike **[main alternative]**, **[Product Name]** **[key difference]**.

Example:

> For operations teams at scaling B2B companies who spend hours each week answering internal questions manually, Acme is the AI knowledge base that gives instant, accurate answers from your company's own documents. Unlike generic chatbots, Acme is trained on your actual content and gets smarter the more your team uses it.
